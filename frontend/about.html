About RoadFix

Built by citizens, for citizens. Empowering communities to report and track road issues in real time.

Our Mission

RoadFix closes the gap between citizens who identify road hazards and maintenance teams who resolve them. We focus on speed, visibility, and accountability across the full issue lifecycle.

Every report includes location context, media proof, and status tracking so communities always know what is happening and when to expect outcomes.

What RoadFix Improves

Faster submissions, transparent workflows, and measurable service quality with built-in role controls.

Report Instantly

Pin exact GPS coordinates, upload photos, and submit complete reports in under a minute.

Track Progress

Follow each issue as it moves from Reported to In Progress and finally to Resolved.

Accountable Workflow

Audit logs and role-based access keep every action verifiable and transparent.
