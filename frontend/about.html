About RoadFix

Built by citizens, for citizens. Empowering communities to report and track road issues in real time.

Our Mission

RoadFix is a civic reporting platform designed to close the gap between citizens who spot road hazards and the maintenance crews who fix them. We believe public infrastructure should be maintained transparently, with real-time accountability for every submitted issue.

Report Instantly

Pin exact GPS coordinates, attach a photo, and submit in under 60 seconds.

Track Progress

Watch your report move from Reported → In Progress → Resolved on the live dashboard.

Accountable System

Role-based access and full audit logs ensure every action is recorded and traceable.
